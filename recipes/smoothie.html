Smoothie

My Fitness Pal Macros

Ingredients

1 banana

75g raspberries

10g Flaxseeds

10g Sesame Seeds

10g Pumpkin Seeds

10g Sunflower Seeds

10g Almonds

10g Walnuts

0.5ml wellbaby vit D drops

500ml Milk

Method

Step 1

Take clean smoothie cup then place one banana split in two inside.

Step 2

Add Raspberries or Blueberries to the cup.

Step 3

Add the 10g of each of the seeds.

Step 4

Add 10g of each of the nuts and 1 drop of Vitamin D to the cup.

Step 5

Add the milk to the cup until almost full with a fimger gap to the top.

Step 6

Firmly seal blender cup and place into blender.

Step 7

Blend and then replace with drinking lid.

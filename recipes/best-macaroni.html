Best Macaroni

Instagram Recipe My Fitness Pal Macros

Ingredients

500g Spirali Pasta

2tbsp Butter

2 tbsp Cornflour

300ml Milk

1tsp Garlic Granules

1/2tsp Onion Powder

1tsp Paprika

1tsp Nutmeg

1tsp Wholegrain Mustard

Salt and Black Pepper

300ml Double Cream

200g Extra Mature Cheddar

250g Red Leicester

200g Mozzarella

(Optional) Stornoway Black Pudding, Haggis, or Sausages

Method

Step 1

Cook pasta in salted water.

Step 2

Melt 2 tbsp Butter in saucepan. Then mix through 2 tbsp flour.

Step 3

300ml milk added in bit by bit mixing through completely before adding next bit (slower is better).

Step 4

1 tsp garlic granules, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp nutmeg (mixed spice), 1 tsp wholegrain mustard, salt and black pepper, (all added before cream).

Step 5

300ml double cream again added bit by bit.

Step 6

250g extra mature cheddar (7 rating), 250g red leceister, 200g mozzarella, all cheese shredded. Again added bit by bit and some left for the top.

Step 7

(Optional add Black Pudding, Haggis, or Sausage.

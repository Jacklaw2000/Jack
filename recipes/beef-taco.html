Beef Taco

My Fitness Pal Macros

Ingredients

500g 5% Beef Steak Mince

4 Wraps

150g Mild Salsa

200g Can of Refried Beans

100g Grated Cheddar

Dressing

200g Natural Yoghurt

20ml Lime Juice

150g Guacamole

Method

Step 1

Brown the mince.

Step 2

Add the salsa and refried beans and cook for another 6-7 mins.

Step 3

Add the cheese.

Step 4

When Serving lay meat mixture on wrap and heat up.

Step 5

Mix Natural yoghurt and guacamole.

Step 6

Add mixture to warm tortilla and meat.
